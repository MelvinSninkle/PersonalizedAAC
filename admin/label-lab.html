Band

Art is its own label

Categories whose tiles get no band — letters, numbers, movie posters, clock faces already ARE their words. Ships in the settings JSON; the production home for this is an own_label flag per taxonomy row, bulk-set by category in the Default board tab.
All sizes are in source pixels on the standard 1024×1024 tile. Text auto-shrinks below the max size until it fits the band. The board clips tiles at 14px radius at ~150px display (≈96px here) — the small preview applies that exact clip, so judge seams there. Nothing here touches production: when a recipe wins, copy the settings JSON into the thread and it becomes the render pipeline.
